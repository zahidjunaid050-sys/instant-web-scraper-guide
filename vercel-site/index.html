Why a website won't scrape: 6 common fixes

When a page gives you no data, or only part of it, the cause is usually simple. Work through these in order.

1. You only got the first few rows

Many pages load more items as you scroll. Scroll to the end first, or use a tool that scrolls for you, then collect the list.

2. The list stops at one page

Look for a Next or Load more button. Your tool needs to follow it, otherwise it only sees the page in front of you.

3. Columns look mixed up

Some items are missing a field, which can shift values. Pick one complete item as your example, and check a few rows against the page.

4. The page can't be read at all

Browsers protect their own pages and add-on stores, so extensions can't read them. Open a normal website tab and try again.

5. Nothing shows until you log in

Data behind a login is only visible in your own session, and the site's terms may limit its use. Check them before you collect anything.

6. Your export has duplicates

Lists often repeat items across pages. Remove duplicate and empty rows before you export.
